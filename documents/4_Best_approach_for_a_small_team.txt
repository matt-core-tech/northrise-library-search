4️⃣ Best approach for a small team

Host the portal online so everyone can access it.

Keep the URL private among your colleagues.

Disable public registration (admin-created accounts).

Optionally, block search engine indexing (noindex).

(Optional) Use password-protect at the hosting level if you want an extra layer of security.

✅ Summary

The system is online, but you control who can log in.

For a small team, share the link privately — no need for it to be searchable.

Even if someone finds the link, they still need credentials to use the system.
